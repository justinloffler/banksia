Group tours & charters

Across town or across the country

Pick from our popular tours or have one tailored to your group. We price most trips as a package, so meals, attractions and entertainment are in with the coach.

Day tours

Up to 250 km · about 8.30–5.30

A leisurely pace, usually within 120 km of Adelaide, with driver commentary, morning tea from the coach, lunch and entry in one price.

Short breaks

2–6 days · up to 750 km

Round trips that never take the same road twice — the Riverland, Port Lincoln, Kangaroo Island, the Grampians, Broken Hill and more.

Extended holidays

7–21 days · anywhere

The Red Centre, Canberra, Sydney and the Blue Mountains, the Gold Coast, Tasmania — with 2–4 night stays built in for a breather.

Camping & safari tours

See the outback when the Sturt peas flower

Worried about gear and supplies? Don't be. Our safaris are fully self-contained and catered — a caravan park one night, a bush camp around the fire the next — and you see the same sights as an accommodated tour for considerably less.

Coaches & drivers

Quiet coaches, drivers who know the roads

Our Volvo and Mercedes coaches seat up to 48 and are kept in tip-top condition, so the hours on board pass quickly.

Cloth reclining seats Heating & air conditioning On-board rest room Reading lamps & curtains Large underfloor luggage bays PA, music & video on board
Our drivers hold hazard licences for the Victorian and NSW alpine areas and know outback dirt roads in all weather. On express runs over 12 hours, two drivers share the wheel with a regulation sleeper bunk on board.
